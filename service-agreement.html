This Professional Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the effective date stated above, by and between RunWave.ai ("Provider") and Jay Campbell, operating as JayCampbell.com ("Client"). Section 1

Scope of Work

Provider agrees to deliver the following services to Client during the term of this Agreement:

1.1 AI-Powered Lead Capture & Nurture Automation

  • Design and implement intelligent lead capture forms and landing page integrations
  • Build automated nurture sequences with behavioral triggers and segmentation
  • Configure lead scoring models to prioritize high-intent prospects
  • Set up multi-channel follow-up workflows (email, SMS where applicable)

1.2 N8N Workflow Builds for Content Repurposing Pipeline

  • Architect and deploy N8N automation workflows for content repurposing
  • Automate content transformation across formats (long-form to social, video to text, etc.)
  • Integrate with Client's existing content platforms and distribution channels
  • Provide workflow documentation and operational guides

1.3 CRM Integration & Automated Follow-Up Sequences

  • Integrate Client's CRM system with lead capture and automation platforms
  • Build automated follow-up sequences triggered by prospect actions and lifecycle stages
  • Configure deal pipeline automation and task assignment rules
  • Ensure data synchronization and integrity across all connected systems

1.4 Landing Page Optimization with Conversion Tracking

  • Audit and optimize existing landing pages for conversion performance
  • Implement conversion tracking pixels and event-based analytics
  • Set up A/B testing frameworks for ongoing optimization
  • Deliver monthly conversion performance reports with actionable recommendations

1.5 Monthly Strategy Calls & Workflow Maintenance

  • One (1) scheduled strategy call per month to review performance and plan next steps
  • Ongoing maintenance of all deployed workflows and automations
  • Bug fixes and adjustments within 48 business hours of reported issues
  • Quarterly roadmap reviews to align services with Client's evolving business goals
Section 2

Deliverables & Timeline

Provider shall deliver the following within the specified timeframes from the Agreement effective date:

  1. Weeks 1–2: Discovery, systems audit, and project roadmap delivery
  2. Weeks 3–4: CRM integration, lead capture setup, and initial workflow deployment
  3. Weeks 5–6: N8N content repurposing pipeline build and testing
  4. Weeks 7–8: Landing page optimization, conversion tracking implementation, and nurture sequence launch
  5. Months 3–6: Ongoing optimization, maintenance, monthly strategy calls, and iterative improvements based on performance data
